Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how LoopTimer – Interval Timer ("the App", "we", "us") collects, uses, and handles information when you use our mobile application.

By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

1. Overview

LoopTimer is an offline-first interval timer app. Its core features — timers, presets, routines, weekly goals, statistics, and custom sounds — work entirely on your device and do not require an account, a login, or an internet connection.

We do not sell your personal data. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or any other personally identifiable information (PII) through the App's own features.

The App integrates third-party services — Google AdMob (advertising) and Google Firebase (analytics and crash reporting) — which may process limited device-level data as described below.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Data stored locally on your device (never sent to us)

All app content and your personal configuration are stored only on your device:

  • Timer presets, routines, and schedules
  • App settings (theme, language, notification preferences)
  • Weekly goals and session statistics/history
  • Sound packs and any custom audio files you import

This data never leaves your device. Deleting the App removes it.

2.2 Analytics (Firebase Analytics)

When you use the App, Firebase Analytics may collect anonymous, aggregated usage events to help us understand how the App is used and improve it. Examples include:

  • Timer stage transitions and completion events
  • Which features/screens are used
  • When ads are shown or fail to load
  • App errors and performance data

These events do not include your name, email, or any data that identifies you personally. They are used in aggregate for product improvement.

2.3 Crash reporting (Firebase Crashlytics)

If the App crashes, Crashlytics may collect technical information to help us fix the problem, including:

  • Device model, OS version, and app version
  • Crash stack traces and logs from the moment of the crash

Crash reports do not contain your personal data.

2.4 Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App is free and supported by advertising. AdMob may collect:

  • Advertising ID (a device-level identifier, e.g., Google Advertising ID / AAID on Android, or IDFA on iOS)
  • Device information and approximate usage of the ad SDK
  • IP address (used for ad delivery and fraud prevention)

Consent: Before ads are personalized, the App shows a Google UMP (User Messaging Platform) consent screen and only requests the Advertising ID after you grant consent. You can:

  • Accept personalized ads
  • Accept only non-personalized ads, or
  • Decline ad data collection entirely (the App remains fully functional)

You can review or change your choice at any time from the App's Settings → Privacy options. On iOS, the App also respects the App Tracking Transparency (ATT) prompt.

2.5 Remote configuration (Firebase Remote Config)

The App may download non-personal configuration values (such as feature flags and timing limits) from Firebase Remote Config. This does not involve your personal data.

3. How We Use Information

We use the information described above to:

  • Operate and maintain the App's features
  • Display advertising that supports the free version of the App
  • Analyze anonymous usage to improve the product
  • Diagnose and fix crashes and errors

We do not use your data for any purpose other than those described in this policy.

  • On-device data: retained until you delete it or uninstall the App.

  • Advertising data: managed by Google AdMob per its retention policies.

6. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will take steps to delete it.

7. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on your location (e.g., GDPR in the EU/EEA, CCPA/CPRA in California), you may have the right to:

  • Access information about the data processed about you
  • Object to or restrict certain processing (e.g., personalized ads)
  • Delete data — since app data is stored on your device, deleting the App or resetting it removes your data; analytics/advertising data can be managed through Google's settings below
  • Opt out of personalized advertising via your device settings (Android: Google Ads settings; iOS: Settings → Privacy → Tracking) or at https://adssettings.google.com

To change or withdraw your ad consent within the App: Settings → Privacy options.

8. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information. Because the App is offline-first and does not transmit your personal data, the primary security risk surface is limited to third-party SDKs (advertising, analytics), which are managed by Google under its own security standards.
