### 4.2 Play Console checklist

- [ ] **Data safety** — declare:
  - App **does not collect personal data** from users.
  - Data collected through the **ad library (AdMob)**: `Device or other IDs` (Advertising ID) — purpose: Advertising; shared with a third party (Google).
  - Data is **not encrypted in transit** / **can't be deleted** — answer the 4 form questions accordingly.
- [ ] **Content rating** — complete the IARC questionnaire: **"Everyone"** (no violence, adult content, gambling...). Answer truthfully; all ages.
- [ ] **Target audience** — choose **13+** (safe because the app contains ads; under 13 requires the Families policy declaration).
- [ ] **Ads** — declare **"Yes, the app contains ads"** (AdMob banner + interstitial + rewarded).
- [ ] **App access** — **"All functionality is accessible free of charge, no login required."**
- [ ] **News apps** — **No**.
- [ ] **Government apps** — **No**.
- [ ] **Declared permissions** — review the permissions declared by the app (from `app.json`):
  - `SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM` — **must be declared in the "Alarms & reminders" form** in Play Console (the app already has a Settings screen guiding users to grant it).
  - `RECORD_AUDIO`, `MODIFY_AUDIO_SETTINGS` — declare purpose: custom alert sounds.
  - `FOREGROUND_SERVICE` / `FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK` — declare purpose: keep the timer running in the background.
  - `RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED` — restore scheduled reminders after device reboot.
  - `AD_ID` — advertising.
- [ ] **Subscriptions / In-app products** — none (free app, ads only). Leave empty.
- [ ] **Store listing extras** — support email + website (if any) in Contact details.

### 4.3 Before submitting

- [ ] Build the **AAB** (currently building on GH Actions) signed with the upload keystore `looptimer-upload.jks` — **keep 2 backups of the keystore + passwords in a safe place** (losing the keystore means you can never update the app).
- [ ] Enable **Play App Signing** on first upload (Google keeps the signing key; you only provide the upload key from your keystore).
- [ ] Verify the AAB's versionCode is ≥ any previously uploaded build (first release = 1).
- [ ] Upload to **internal/closed testing** first to test on a real device, then promote to production.
